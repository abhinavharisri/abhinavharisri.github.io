What GPay gets right

Five small things on one home screen, and the lesson underneath them.

2 min read

The main job of GPay, PhonePe or Paytm is simple: daily payments. The thing they try hardest to make easy is finishing a payment in seconds. Looking at GPay's home screen with that in mind, a few choices stand out.

  1. The main actions come firstScan a QR, pay anyone, bank transfer, mobile recharge. Right in front of you, no searching.
  2. Frequent people are on the home screenIf you pay someone every day, they're one tap away.
  3. Bills and recharges get their own sectionYou don't hunt for them. They're where you expect them.
  4. Rewards live separatelyGift cards, offers and cashback have a place without crowding the payment flow.
  5. Balance sits next to money managementChecking what you have and managing it go hand in hand.
Products do well when related things are grouped together, so the user never has to think about where to look.

The annoying part

The one thing that still feels slow is the payment itself on a bad day: slow bank servers and patchy connectivity. The app accepts that friction because most of it isn't in its hands. Payments need the internet, and the slow part is often the bank, not the app.

That's exactly why I think the biggest product wins in payments are about reliability, not features.

Product thinking means noticing the small flaws a regular person never thinks about, instead of just using the app.
