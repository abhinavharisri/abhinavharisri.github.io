About

Abhinav Harisri is the founder of hundrd, a Bengaluru studio that designs, builds and hosts fast, secure websites for businesses that operate at scale, down to the hundredth detail.

The path there wasn't a straight line. Abhinav started out in cybersecurity, learning how systems break, then got pulled toward a different question: how money moves. Payments, risk, markets. That curiosity led to fundraising work at a sports-tech startup, finance work at a manufacturer, and a habit of tracking the market every month.

Alongside the studio, Abhinav is open to internships in finance, research and analytics.
