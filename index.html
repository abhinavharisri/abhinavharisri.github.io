About

I'm Abhinav. I run hundrd, a Bengaluru studio that designs, builds and hosts fast, secure websites for businesses that operate at scale, down to the hundredth detail.

The path here wasn't a straight line. I started out in cybersecurity, learning how systems break, then got pulled toward a different question: how money moves. Payments, risk, markets. That curiosity led to fundraising work at a sports-tech startup, finance work at a manufacturer, and a growing pile of notes on how payments actually work.

Alongside the studio, I'm open to internships in finance, research and analytics.
